This is the ESG report for the company "Open Source AI Ltd"

Environment

Open Source AI Ltd is committed to reducing greenhouse gases by 75% by the year 2035, including a 100% reduction of Scope 1 emissions, and a 50% reduction in Scope 2 and Scope 3 emissions.

Social

Open Source AI Ltd has implemented programmes to promote diversity, equity and inclusion within the workplace

Governance

Open Source AI Ltd is training all employees in areas such as Anti-corruption, Whisteblowing and GDPR.
